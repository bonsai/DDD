# Research and Experiment

**Type:** research
**Status:** accepted
**Source Issues:** #8 #19 #20

Research reduces uncertainty by collecting and synthesizing existing knowledge. Experiment reduces uncertainty through controlled observation.

## Research note

- Issue / question
- scope
- sources
- findings
- synthesis
- confidence
- unresolved questions

## Experiment report

- hypothesis
- setup
- variables
- procedure
- result
- interpretation
- limitations
- evidence generated
- next Issue

Research and Experiment are evidence-producing documents, not decisions by themselves.
