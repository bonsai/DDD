# Evidence

**Type:** evidence
**Status:** accepted
**Source Issues:** #8 #15 #20

Evidence records what supports a claim and how reliable that support is.

## Minimum fields

- claim
- source
- date
- method
- observation/result
- confidence
- limitations
- related Issue/Document

## Epistemic rule

`accepted` means organizationally adopted, not necessarily objectively true. Evidence and confidence must remain explicit.

Research, experiments, tests, observations, and feedback may all produce Evidence.
